Who We Are – Robo Forge FTC Team

We are Robo Forge, an FTC robotics team from the 1 Vocational High School of Nikiti, Greece. United by our passion for technology, engineering, and innovation, we work together to transform our ideas into reality. As a team, we design, build, and program robots, constantly challenging ourselves to learn new skills, solve problems, and improve our abilities.

For us, FTC is more than a robotics competition. It is an opportunity to develop teamwork, creativity, leadership, and critical thinking while sharing our enthusiasm for STEM. Every challenge teaches us something new, and every achievement motivates us to aim higher. This is our second year participating in FTC, we are determined to grow, push our limits, and represent our school with pride.

At Robo Forge, we believe that great ideas become reality when passion, teamwork, and determination come together.
